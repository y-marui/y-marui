# Improved Panel for Google Scholar – Explore Faster

Read Google Scholar recommendations with fewer repeated clicks.

## Features
- Automatically loads more recommended papers.
- Automatically expands paper abstracts.
- Adds period headers with collapsible groups.
- Expands or collapses abstracts together from the toolbar popup.
- Opens paper titles and full-text links in background tabs.
- Offers an option to skip saved papers when expanding abstracts automatically.

## Getting started
After installation, open https://scholar.google.com/scholar?sciupd=1. Reload the page if it is already open. Use the settings page to control automatic loading, abstract expansion, handling of saved papers, and link behavior.

## Scope and limitations
This extension is designed for the recommended-papers page, not regular search results. The extension interface supports Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, French, and Portuguese. It does not change Google Scholar’s own language. Date parsing for period grouping supports Japanese and English date labels. Changes to Google Scholar may affect functionality.

## Privacy
Settings are stored locally in your browser. The extension does not send paper information or browsing history to its developer and does not include analytics. Interacting with Google Scholar or opening paper links makes normal requests to the respective websites.

This is an independently developed extension and is not provided or endorsed by Google.
