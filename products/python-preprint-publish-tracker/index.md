# Preprint Publish Tracker

Register the preprints you follow (arXiv, bioRxiv, medRxiv, Research Square, TechRxiv and others) and get an email when one is published as a journal article with a DOI.

## Access

The service is invitation only and is limited to people with an `.ac.jp` email address. There is no public sign-up. The [front page](https://preprint.ymarui.com/) explains how it works and is readable without an account.
