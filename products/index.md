# Products

## Browser Extensions

### [Focus Scope - Smart Website Blocker](chrome-focus-scope/) [![Chrome](https://img.shields.io/badge/Chrome-4285F4?logo=googlechrome&logoColor=white)](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/dnpccglkhnnlogdolajjhehicbegnlcg) [![Firefox](https://img.shields.io/badge/Firefox-FF7139?logo=firefoxbrowser&logoColor=white)](https://addons.mozilla.org/ja/firefox/addon/focus-scope/)

Block SNS and time-wasting sites. A site blocker to keep your focus going with sprints and day-of-week schedules.

### [Improved Panel for Google Scholar – Explore Faster](chrome-improved-panel-for-google-scholar/) [![Chrome](https://img.shields.io/badge/Chrome-4285F4?logo=googlechrome&logoColor=white)](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/cihbkinmpknihmjklabiepkbahmnhopi)

Read Google Scholar recommendations with fewer repeated clicks. Firefox is not published on Add-ons. Future Firefox support remains under consideration.

### [Library Check for Zotero – Prevent Duplicates](chrome-library-check-for-zotero/) [![Chrome](https://img.shields.io/badge/Chrome-4285F4?logo=googlechrome&logoColor=white)](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/njakhbdjddaflodiogjmbledldjjbbai) [![Firefox](https://img.shields.io/badge/Firefox-FF7139?logo=firefoxbrowser&logoColor=white)](https://addons.mozilla.org/ja/firefox/addon/library-check-for-zotero/)

Check instantly whether the paper you're viewing is already saved in Zotero. A simple tool to prevent duplicate saves.

### [Pick Engine – Route Your Searches](chrome-pick-engine/) [![Chrome](https://img.shields.io/badge/Chrome-4285F4?logo=googlechrome&logoColor=white)](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ihcfdhceinllkcdopimkbofloecbgdgc)

Splits your address-bar search across the search engines you choose, with no external relay.

## Apps

### [Glance Cal - Tasks and events as widgets](swift-glance-cal/) ![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-not%20yet%20published-orange)

View your Google Calendar events and Google Tasks from macOS and iOS widgets. The app itself also lets you create, edit, and delete events and tasks.

### [Glance Task - Your tasks, always in glance](swift-glance-task/) ![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-not%20yet%20published-orange)

View and edit your Google Tasks from macOS and iOS widgets, and add tasks with Siri.

### [Stick Mark - Sticky Markdown Notes](stick-mark/) ![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-not%20yet%20published-orange)

Markdown notes for Mac and iPhone. Show a note as a desktop widget, and sync with iCloud.

## Web Apps

### [Preprint Publish Tracker](python-preprint-publish-tracker/) [![Web App](https://img.shields.io/badge/Web%20App-invitation%20only-555555)](https://preprint.ymarui.com/)

Get an email when a preprint you follow is published as a journal article with a DOI. Invitation only, for people with an .ac.jp address.

Google Workspace Add-on that merges paired calendar events, such as clock-in and clock-out events.
